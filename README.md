# atliq-sales-finance-analytics
Excel-based Sales &amp; Finance Analytics project for AtliQ Hardware — Power Query, Pivot Tables, and DAX

**AtliQ Hardware – Sales & Finance Performance Analysis (Excel)**

Domain: Sales Analytics & Finance | Tools: Advanced Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, DAX, Pivot Tables, Conditional Formatting

🏢** Business Context**

AtliQ Hardware manufactures and sells PCs, printers, and accessories across global markets, distributing through retail partners (Croma, Best Buy) and e-commerce platforms (Amazon, Flipkart). The business needed structured reporting to track revenue growth, product performance, customer behavior, and financial health across multiple fiscal years.

🎯 Objective

Design Excel-based Sales and Finance reports delivering insight into revenue growth, customer performance, market profitability, division-level contribution, target-vs-actual performance, and overall financial health.

📈 Sales Analysis

Reports Developed

Customer Net Sales Performance (2019–2021)
Market Performance vs Sales Target
Division-Level Sales Report (2020 & 2021)
New Products Performance (2021)
Top 5 Countries by Revenue (2021)

Key Metrics & Insights

Revenue grew from $87.5M (2019) to $598.9M (2021)
P&A division revenue grew from $105.2M (2020) to $338.4M (2021); PC division also posted strong YoY growth
16 new products launched in 2021, contributing $176.2M in sales
Top new products: AQ Qwerty ($22M), AQ Trigger ($20.7M), AQ Gen Y ($19.5M)
India led all markets at $161.3M, followed by the USA at $87.8M
Most markets grew YoY; New Zealand, Norway, and Austria declined
Several customers recorded >300% YoY growth, flagging strong account-level opportunities

💰 Finance Analysis

**Reports Developed**
Yearly Profit & Loss (P&L)
Monthly P&L Comparison
Market-Specific P&L
Quarterly Gross Margin % by Sub-Zone

Key Metrics & Insights

Net sales grew 204.5% over three fiscal years (2019–2021)
Gross margin % held relatively stable despite rising operational costs
Profitability varied meaningfully by region — sub-zone GM% comparison (FY19–FY21) supported benchmarking and optimization planning across ANZ, India, NA, NE, ROA, and SE
🛠 Technical Implementation
Data cleaning & ETL via Power Query
Relational data model: fact_sales_monthly linked to dim_customer, dim_market, dim_product, dim_date
Custom fiscal calendar (months & quarters)
DAX calculated columns and CALCULATE()-based dynamic filtering
Target vs. actual variance analysis
Conditional formatting for fast visual interpretation

📌 Business Impact
Identified high-performing customers to inform discount and negotiation strategy
Surfaced high-potential international markets for expansion
Improved financial visibility to support strategic, region-level planning
Increased transparency and consistency in performance reporting across teams
